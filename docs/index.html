Ports the verified formulas from the Flow-coef-calc library. Liquid: turbulent sizing (clause 6.1) and the choked drop Δpchoked = FL²(p₁ − FF pv) (clause 7). Gas: the compressible equation with expansion factor Y = 1 − x/(3 Fγ xT), choking at x ≥ FγxT (clause 7). The constant N₇ = 455.3 is derived, not quoted. Cv = Kv/0.865.

Pinned to the standard's own worked examples across 331 tests. A demonstration, not a substitute for a stamped sizing calculation. Gas xT is a datasheet value; the library reads it off the valve's published curve and iterates.
